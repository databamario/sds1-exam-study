Read L3: three claims the lecture never makes, and two untaught arguments

Eight questions. Five passed, three were wrong, and the three share a shape:
they ask about something the lecture simply does not cover.

L3-WA-008 asked why a histogram is preferred to a bar plot for a numerical
variable. Lecture 3 does not teach bar plots - the word appears once, on p.54,
in a warning about geom_col(). What p.24 actually contrasts is a histogram with
a dotplot, so that is what the question now asks.

L3-MC-016 keyed "NA with a warning" as the result of mean() on c(1, 2, "3"),
where p.58 prints "# error" and then the value NA. L3-WA-003's answer had the
same phrase. Both now say NA and stop there, which is what the page shows; the
distinction the question is really testing is NA against a number, not NA
against a hard stop.

L1-WA-007 and L3-MC-010 both lean on the interquartile range, which Lecture 3
never defines. It is named on p.13 in a list of topics and again on p.66, and
nowhere else. L3-MC-010 now keys Q3 minus Q1, which p.35 states outright, and
its detail says so.

The rest were pages that existed and had not been cited. `.by` is on p.54
(L2-MC-017). The interquartile range's basis, the quartiles, are on p.35
(L3-MC-007). The type hierarchy is on p.56, while p.58 is only the pitfall
(L3-WA-003). L3-MC-001 cites the p.38 recap beside p.17, because what it lacked
was not the mean but the mode, range and standard deviation its explanation
names.

That last one is the useful finding. I reached for p.15, the mean page, because
option (c) is about the mean being pulled upward - and the gate refused it. It
was right. The terms actually missing after p.17 were mode, range, sd and
standard, and those live on the recap table. The page that looked obviously
correct was not the page the question needed, and term overlap said so before I
did.

Five of nineteen wrong. Fourteen passed on the same test: L1-WA-001 maps all
four sampling terms onto p.26, L1-WA-004 restates p.44's rule about the hash
symbol, L2-MC-011's two keyed options are the positional indices p.48 teaches,
L2-MC-016's follow from p.54's printed pipeline, L3-MC-019's from p.62's
description of the left join, and L3-WA-001's three formulas are the p.38 recap
verbatim.

Suite green apart from the rebuilt bank being uncommitted.
