Read L7, L8 and L9: the last unreviewed questions, and two rubrics grading nothing

Fourteen questions. Three were wrong, three needed a second page, eight passed.

L9-WA-008 keyed "linear regression and a fuller treatment of correlation are
deferred to SDS2". Searching Lecture 9 for SDS2 returns nothing, and its final
three pages are the exam notice and the course evaluation. The lecture never
says it, so the question and the rubric point that graded it are gone; what
remains is the half the lecture does support.

L9-WA-003 and L9-WA-006 had the same defect in miniature. Each rubric graded an
element its own keyed answer never contained - that the bootstrap approximates
rather than computes, and that the simulation converges to the analytic value as
N grows. A student who answered the key perfectly and stopped there would have
lost those points for saying nothing extra. Both answers now contain the clause
their rubrics were already asking for, which is the whole point of a rubric.

L8-MC-008 named Tukey as the post hoc test, and Tukey is printed on exactly one
page of Lecture 8, the p.40 flow chart, while the omnibus argument is on p.25.
It now cites both. L7-WA-006's answer closes on the paired-data rule, which is
p.52 rather than the p.47 it cited, and L9-WA-008 now also cites p.12 for its
Monte-Carlo half.

### The eighth false alarm, and this time the reader was mine

L7-MC-009 looked like the worst finding of the review. Option (c) is p.47's
formula written out character for character, and it read as marked wrong while a
prose paraphrase of the same thing was marked right - a keying error on a
question students get wrong for free. I had the write-up half-formed.

The bank disagreed. Its correct list is ['a', 'c']. Both were keyed correctly
from the start, and the viewer I wrote earlier in this pass had printed KEY> on
both lines; I misread the block rather than checking it.

That is the eighth misread snapshot in this review and the first where the
faulty reader was one I had just written. It is also the clearest argument for
the rule that has caught all eight: read the key from tools/bank/ or from
questions.json, never from a rendered dump, however carefully the dump is
formatted.

Every one of the 213 L-* questions has now been read against its slides. Suite
green apart from the rebuilt bank being uncommitted.
