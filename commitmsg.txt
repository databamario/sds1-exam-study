Give the eight cut questions their second page back

The three Band F batches all found the same defect: the answer needed a second
page, the format allowed one, so the fix was to delete real course content. Six
times in twenty. The two-page citations landed in the previous commit; this puts
the content back.

L3-WA-004 contrasts an inner join with a left join again, and L3-WA-006 asks for
a measure of spread as well as the shape of the distribution. L4-MC-011 keys the
conditional definition of independence, which is p.29 and not p.30 where the
product rule lives, and L5-MC-010 gets var(X-bar) = sigma squared over n as an
option of its own. L6-MC-003 gets the rule that about 95% of sample means fall
within two standard errors, and L6-WA-004 gets its basketball club example back
together with the name the lecture gives it, coverage bias. L8-WA-003 asks for
the df = k - 1 rule, and L8-WA-006 asks for both the independence and the
homogeneity chi-square, which p.40's flow chart cannot tell apart.

L1-MC-019 was on the same list and deliberately did not get one. Its batch 3
repair had replaced an option keying a claim about right-hand-side evaluation,
which no page of Lecture 1 makes. The replacement states what p.52 carries, and
p.52's function row says all of it - "can be assigned or passed as values", with
function(x) x^2 as its example, the same code the question runs. Nothing was
missing. Adding a page would have satisfied the new gate for the wrong reason,
which is the failure mode the gate exists to catch, so it would have been silly
to walk into it deliberately.

The gate's weakness was measured instead of guessed at. It passes an extra page
that supplies one incidental word, and L1-MC-019 was exactly that: p.41 gives
`lists` and nothing else, 5% of the weight. The tempting fix was a threshold, and
the numbers refuse it. L3-WA-004's p.61 also supplies a single word at 8%, but
that word is `inner` and it is the whole reason the page is there - `join` is too
common across Lecture 3 for the scorer to count. A cut at 10% would fail a
question whose second page is genuinely load-bearing. So the bar stays low and the
gate now prints what every extra page supplied, which makes a passing run
readable instead of merely green. Passing is not evidence the page was needed,
and the tool now says so where someone will see it.

The format gate caught three things in this batch and all three were mine: a
five-option question with three correct answers, twice, and two written answers
that ran past the word limit. Also two explanations that said "the lecture",
which validate.py forbids.

Suite green apart from the rebuilt bank being uncommitted.
